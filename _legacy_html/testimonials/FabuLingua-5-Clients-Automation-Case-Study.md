# **Case Study: FabuLingua (5 New Clients) | In 48 Days**

---

#### **Client Overview**

Fabulingua is an innovative educational platform focused on teaching languages to kids through storytelling and interactive learning. They wanted to accelerate their client acquisition by connecting directly with their ideal customers.

#### **Challenge**

Fabulingua faced difficulties scaling their outreach and consistently connecting with the right decision-makers and educators interested in their platform. Their existing inbound methods were slow and unpredictable in delivering qualified leads.

#### **Solution**

AutoIkigaiSystems partnered with Fabulingua to design and implement a targeted automated sales system aimed at their Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). The approach included:

* **Defining the ICP:** Identifying key personas such as schools, language learning centers, and parents actively seeking engaging educational tools.

* **Creating Personalized Outreach at Scale:** Developing tailored messaging and multi-step automated campaigns to initiate meaningful conversations.

* **Sales Automation Infrastructure:** Implementing systems to manage lead capture, follow-ups, and pipeline tracking efficiently.

* **Intelligent Lead Nurturing:** Setting up automated processes to qualify and nurture prospects, guiding them smoothly toward conversion.

#### **Results 🚀**

Within 48 days of launching the automated system, Fabulingua achieved:

* **5 New Clients Acquired:** Directly resulting from targeted automated outreach and effective engagement.

* **$8,288 Initial Investment:** The automation setup and campaign execution delivered a strong return on investment.

* **Faster Pipeline Movement:** Qualified leads progressed swiftly through automated nurture sequences, reducing sales cycle time.

* **A Scalable Sales System:** Fabulingua now has a repeatable, automated outbound process to fuel ongoing growth.
