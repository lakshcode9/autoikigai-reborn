Frequently Asked Questions

Do you integrate with our CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive)?

Yes, we support all major CRMs plus CSV uploads. We'll set up the integration during onboarding so qualified leads flow directly into your system.

What counts as a qualified booking for solar?

We agree on criteria upfront: roof condition, homeowner status, minimum electric bill, timeline, and service area. Only leads meeting all criteria get booked.

How fast can we see bookings?

Typically within days of campaign launch. Most clients see their first qualified booking within 3-7 days, with consistent volume building over the first 2-3 weeks.

How do you handle opt-outs and TCPA/DNC compliance?

Consent-first outreach with opt-out on every message. We honor opt-outs instantly and maintain DNC lists. Local caller IDs where applicable for better response rates.

Absolutely. We recommend starting with 500-1000 leads to test and optimize the flow before scaling. This lets you see results quickly while minimizing risk.
