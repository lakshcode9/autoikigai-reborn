# 🧹 Loom Script — Commercial Janitorial

> **Duration:** 90 seconds max
> **Format:** Face-on-camera + screen share demo
> **Tone:** Business-savvy, contract-focused — these are recurring revenue plays
> **DO NOT mention:** Pricing, success fees, or specific dollar amounts

---

## Pre-Recording Setup
1. Open Loom → select "Screen + Camera"
2. Camera: bottom-left corner, your face visible throughout
3. Have ready on screen:
   - A mock Salesmessage dashboard showing SMS conversations
   - A spreadsheet showing "lost bids" and "expired contracts"
4. Dress: casual professional
5. Background: clean, professional

---

## Script

### [0-10 seconds] — THE HOOK
> *Direct to camera, slightly incredulous tone*

**"Quick question for any janitorial company owner — when you lose a bid or a contract expires, what happens to that contact? It just sits in a spreadsheet somewhere, right? That's a recurring revenue contract you're sitting on."**

---

### [10-30 seconds] — THE PROBLEM + PROOF
> *Transition to screen share — spreadsheet of old facility contacts*

**"Here's what I see with janitorial companies all the time. You bid on a building, maybe you lose to someone cheaper, or the contract expires and the facility manager switches providers. You move on to the next bid.**

**But here's what nobody thinks about — facility managers change. The person who picked your competitor? They probably left that role 6 months later. The new FM inherited a cleaning company they didn't choose, and they might not even like them."**

---

### [30-60 seconds] — THE DEMO
> *Show screen: Salesmessage SMS conversation*

**"So we take your old contacts — lost bids, expired contracts, everyone who ever said 'not right now' — and we re-engage them automatically.**

*[Point to screen]* **Here's what that looks like: 'Hi David, this is [Company Name]. We worked with your building a while back on janitorial services. Quick question — are you happy with your current cleaning crew, or is that something worth revisiting?'**

**Simple, right? No pressure. But the response rate is wild — because a lot of these people are actually frustrated with their current provider. They just never had anyone reach back out."**

---

### [60-80 seconds] — THE OUTCOME
> *Back to face cam*

**"Keep in mind — one janitorial contract is worth what, $3K to $10K a month? Recurring. So even if we bring back ONE old contact, that could be $36K to $120K in annual revenue.**

**We typically see 15-20% of old contacts respond, and a good percentage of those are open to switching. This isn't cold outreach — these are people who already know your company."**

---

### [80-90 seconds] — THE CTA
> *Lean in, peer-to-peer close*

**"I pulled some data on janitorial companies in your area and I think there's a real opportunity here. Want me to show you what we'd do with your specific list? Just reply — happy to walk you through it."**

> *Hold eye contact, end recording*

---

## Post-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Video is under 90 seconds
- [ ] Recurring revenue angle emphasized (not one-time jobs)
- [ ] Facility manager turnover is mentioned (key insight)
- [ ] No pricing mentioned
- [ ] Custom thumbnail: your face + text "Your Old Janitorial Contacts"
- [ ] CTA is soft — reply, not "book a call"
