# 😴 Loom Script — Sleep Apnea Clinics

> **Duration:** 90 seconds max
> **Format:** Face-on-camera + screen share demo
> **Tone:** Professional, empathetic — patient health is involved, respect it
> **DO NOT mention:** Pricing, success fees, or specific dollar amounts
> **IMPORTANT:** Be HIPAA-conscious in demo — no real patient data, use obviously fake names

---

## Pre-Recording Setup
1. Open Loom → select "Screen + Camera"
2. Camera: bottom-left corner, your face visible throughout
3. Have ready on screen:
   - A mock Salesmessage dashboard showing SMS conversations
   - A spreadsheet showing "inactive patients" (obviously fake data)
4. Dress: slightly more professional (button-up recommended — medical audience)
5. Background: clean, professional

---

## Script

### [0-10 seconds] — THE HOOK
> *Direct to camera, genuine concern*

**"If you run a sleep apnea clinic, here's a number that should bother you — about 50-60% of patients who get prescribed a CPAP stop using it within the first year. Those patients don't disappear. They're still in your system. They still can't sleep. They just stopped coming in."**

---

### [10-30 seconds] — THE PROBLEM + PROOF
> *Transition to screen share — spreadsheet of "inactive patients"*

**"Every sleep clinic I've talked to has the same problem — hundreds of patients who came in for a study, got their diagnosis, maybe tried a CPAP for a month, and then fell off. Your front desk is too busy with new patients to chase down people from 6 months ago.**

**But those patients still have sleep apnea. It didn't go away. And some of them have probably been meaning to come back — they just need someone to make it easy."**

---

### [30-60 seconds] — THE DEMO
> *Show screen: Salesmessage SMS conversation*

**"So what we do is take your list of inactive or lapsed patients and send a simple re-engagement sequence on your behalf.**

*[Point to screen]* **Here's an example: 'Hi Michael, this is [Clinic Name]. We wanted to check in — it's been a while since your last visit and we have some new treatment options that a lot of patients are finding more comfortable than traditional CPAP. Would you like to schedule a follow-up?'**

**See how gentle that is? No pressure. Just a check-in. And the response rates on this are really strong — because these patients actually WANT help. They just fell through the cracks."**

---

### [60-80 seconds] — THE OUTCOME
> *Back to face cam*

**"On average, we see 20-25% of lapsed patients respond to sequences like this. And in the sleep apnea space, each reactivated patient represents significant lifetime value — multiple follow-ups, equipment, adjustments, new masks.**

**We're not finding you new patients. We're bringing back the ones you already have — the ones who already trust your clinic."**

---

### [80-90 seconds] — THE CTA
> *Professional, warm close*

**"I've been looking into sleep clinics in your area and I think there's a real opportunity to re-engage your patient base. Would it be worth a quick conversation? Just reply to this email — happy to show you what this would look like for your specific clinic."**

> *Hold eye contact, end recording*

---

## Post-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Video is under 90 seconds
- [ ] Tone is empathetic and professional (medical audience)
- [ ] Patient health framing (not just "revenue")
- [ ] No real patient data shown on screen — all obviously fake/demo
- [ ] CPAP abandonment stat mentioned (builds credibility)
- [ ] "New treatment options" angle used (not just "come back")
- [ ] No pricing mentioned
- [ ] Custom thumbnail: your face + text "Your Inactive Sleep Patients"
- [ ] CTA is soft — reply, not "book a call"
