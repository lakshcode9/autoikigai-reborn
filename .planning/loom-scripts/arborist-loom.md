# 🌳 Loom Script — Arborist / Tree Care

> **Duration:** 90 seconds max
> **Format:** Face-on-camera + screen share demo
> **Tone:** Friendly, seasonal urgency — trees don't fix themselves
> **DO NOT mention:** Pricing, success fees, or specific dollar amounts

---

## Pre-Recording Setup
1. Open Loom → select "Screen + Camera"
2. Camera: bottom-left corner, your face visible throughout
3. Have ready on screen:
   - A mock Salesmessage dashboard showing SMS conversations
   - A spreadsheet of "old estimates" with tree removal / trimming notes
4. Dress: casual professional
5. Background: clean, professional

---

## Script

### [0-10 seconds] — THE HOOK
> *Direct to camera, relatable energy*

**"If you run a tree care company, think about this — every estimate you sent last year for a tree removal or a big trim job... that tree is still there. The homeowner still looks at it every single day. They just never pulled the trigger."**

---

### [10-30 seconds] — THE PROBLEM + PROOF
> *Transition to screen share — spreadsheet of old estimates*

**"Here's what happens in tree care — you go out, you give the estimate, maybe it's $2,000 for a big oak removal. The homeowner says 'let me think about it.' And that's the last you hear from them.**

**But that tree didn't go anywhere. And now storm season is coming, or their HOA is sending letters, or a branch fell on their neighbor's fence. They need it done — they just need a nudge."**

---

### [30-60 seconds] — THE DEMO
> *Show screen: Salesmessage SMS conversation*

**"So what we do is take all your old estimates — every homeowner who said 'maybe later' — and we send them a simple follow-up sequence automatically.**

*[Point to screen]* **Look at this: 'Hey Lisa, this is [Company Name]. Quick question — that maple tree we looked at back in September, is that still something you want to take care of? We've got a crew in your neighborhood next week and could fit you in.'**

**That's it. No hard sell. Just a reminder that you exist and you're available. And people respond to this because the problem never went away — they just forgot to call you back."**

---

### [60-80 seconds] — THE OUTCOME
> *Back to face cam*

**"We typically see 15-20% response rates on these. And tree care jobs are high-ticket — $800, $2,000, $5,000. So if you've got 150 old estimates and we bring back even 10-15 jobs, that's potentially $15K to $50K in revenue from your existing customer base.**

**No new leads needed. No advertising. Just working your own list."**

---

### [80-90 seconds] — THE CTA
> *Warm, friendly close*

**"I actually looked into some tree care companies in your area and I think there's a lot of low-hanging fruit here — pun intended. Want me to show you what we'd do with your estimates? Just reply to this email."**

> *Smile, hold eye contact, end recording*

---

## Post-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Video is under 90 seconds
- [ ] Seasonal urgency mentioned (storm season, HOA pressure)
- [ ] "The tree is still there" hook landed in first 10 seconds
- [ ] No pricing mentioned
- [ ] Custom thumbnail: your face + text "Your Old Tree Estimates"
- [ ] CTA is soft — reply, not "book a call"
- [ ] Pun at the end is optional but recommended (adds personality)
