# 🔥 Loom Script — Commercial Fire & Security

> **Duration:** 90 seconds max
> **Format:** Face-on-camera + screen share demo
> **Tone:** Urgent but helpful — compliance angle creates natural pressure
> **DO NOT mention:** Pricing, success fees, or specific dollar amounts

---

## Pre-Recording Setup
1. Open Loom → select "Screen + Camera"
2. Camera: bottom-left corner, your face visible throughout
3. Have ready on screen:
   - A mock Salesmessage dashboard showing SMS conversations
   - A simple spreadsheet showing fire safety inspection quotes going stale
4. Dress: casual professional
5. Background: clean, professional

---

## Script

### [0-10 seconds] — THE HOOK
> *Look directly into camera, slightly concerned expression*

**"If you're in the fire and security space, I need to show you something. Every expired quote sitting in your system? That's a building that's probably still out of compliance. And someone else is going to close that deal if you don't."**

---

### [10-30 seconds] — THE PROBLEM + PROOF
> *Transition to screen share — spreadsheet of "expired quotes"*

**"Here's what I keep seeing with fire and security companies — you do the inspection, you send the quote for the alarm upgrade or the extinguisher service, and then... crickets. The building manager gets busy, the quote expires, and it just sits there.**

**But the compliance deadline didn't go away. That building still needs their fire panel serviced. The FM is probably getting pressure from their insurer right now."**

---

### [30-60 seconds] — THE DEMO
> *Show screen: Salesmessage SMS conversation*

**"So what we do is we take all those expired quotes and old leads and we run an automated re-engagement sequence.**

*[Point to screen]* **Look — here's a text that goes out: 'Hi Sarah, quick question from [Company Name] — we had your building at 220 Main St on our schedule for a fire panel update. Is that still something you need taken care of? We've got a technician in your area next week.'**

**See how that works? It's not salesy — it's a reminder. And because it's compliance-related, people actually respond. They HAVE to get this done."**

---

### [60-80 seconds] — THE OUTCOME
> *Back to face cam*

**"The response rates on fire and security reactivation are some of the highest we see — because this isn't optional for them. It's compliance.**

**We typically see 20-25% of these old leads respond, and a solid chunk of those turn into booked service calls. That's revenue your team already earned the right to close."**

---

### [80-90 seconds] — THE CTA
> *Casual, confident close*

**"I actually did some homework on your area and I think there's some easy wins here. Want me to show you what that would look like for your specific leads? Just reply to this email — takes 2 minutes."**

> *Hold eye contact, end recording*

---

## Post-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Video is under 90 seconds
- [ ] Compliance angle is front and center (not generic "follow up" pitch)
- [ ] No pricing mentioned
- [ ] Demo screen shows a believable fire/security scenario (panel update, extinguisher service, etc.)
- [ ] Custom thumbnail: your face + text "Your Expired Fire Safety Quotes"
- [ ] CTA is soft — reply, not "book a call"
