# 🛣️ Loom Script — Commercial Paving & Sealcoating

> **Duration:** 90 seconds max
> **Format:** Face-on-camera + screen share demo
> **Tone:** Casual, peer-to-peer, like a buddy who figured something out
> **DO NOT mention:** Pricing, success fees, or specific dollar amounts

---

## Pre-Recording Setup
1. Open Loom → select "Screen + Camera"
2. Camera: bottom-left corner, your face visible throughout
3. Have ready on screen:
   - A mock Salesmessage dashboard showing SMS conversations
   - A simple spreadsheet showing "Before/After" lead status
4. Dress: casual professional (polo/button-up, no suit)
5. Background: clean, professional (home office or neutral wall)

---

## Script

### [0-10 seconds] — THE HOOK
> *Look directly into camera, slight lean forward*

**"Hey — quick question. How many paving quotes did your team send out last year that never turned into jobs? 50? 100? 200?**

**That's a LOT of money just sitting in your CRM, right?"**

---

### [10-30 seconds] — THE PROBLEM + PROOF
> *Transition to screen share showing a mock CRM with "dead" leads*

**"Here's what happens at most paving companies — you send the quote, the customer goes cold, and nobody follows up more than once or twice. Your guys are too busy actually doing the work to chase old leads.**

**But here's the thing — a lot of those people STILL need their driveway done. They just got busy, or the timing wasn't right, or they forgot."**

---

### [30-60 seconds] — THE DEMO
> *Show screen: Salesmessage SMS dashboard with automated conversation*

**"So what we do is we take your list of old quotes — everyone who ghosted you — and we run an automated follow-up sequence for you.**

*[Point to screen]* **See this? This is a text that goes out to your old leads. It says something like: 'Hey John, this is Mike's Paving. Quick question — are you still looking to get that parking lot sealed? We have some crew availability this month.'**

**The responses come back automatically. People say 'yes, actually,' or 'what would it cost now?' — and we route those straight to your team to book the job."**

---

### [60-80 seconds] — THE OUTCOME
> *Back to face cam, confident but not pushy*

**"On average, we're seeing about 15-20% of old leads actually respond, and about 5-10% of those turn into booked appointments.**

**So if you've got 200 old quotes sitting there, that could be 10-20 jobs you'd have never gotten otherwise. All from leads you already had."**

---

### [80-90 seconds] — THE CTA
> *Lean in slightly, casual close*

**"I actually started pulling some data on your area already — looks like there's a few things we could do. Want me to walk you through it? Just reply to this email or shoot me a text. Either way, no pressure — just thought you should see this."**

> *Hold eye contact for 1 second, then end recording*

---

## Post-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Video is under 90 seconds
- [ ] Your face is visible the entire time
- [ ] No pricing mentioned
- [ ] Demo screen is clear and readable
- [ ] Custom Loom thumbnail shows your face + text "Your Old Paving Quotes"
- [ ] CTA is soft (reply/text), not "book a call"
