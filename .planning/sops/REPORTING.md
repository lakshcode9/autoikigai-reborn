### Key Metrics to Watch
- **Client LTV:** Average revenue per client over their lifetime
- **Churn rate:** % of clients who cancel after month 1
- **Avg appointments/client/month:** Target 5-15
- **Cost per appointment delivered:** Target <$10
- **Net margin per client/month:** Target >70%

### Health Indicators
| Indicator | 🟢 Good | 🟡 Warning | 🔴 Problem |
|-----------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Response rate | >20% | 10-20% | <10% |
| Appointment rate | >5% | 3-5% | <3% |
| Client communication | Weekly reports on time | Missed one report | >2 weeks no contact |
| Payment collection | Paid within Net 15 | Paid within Net 30 | >30 days overdue |
