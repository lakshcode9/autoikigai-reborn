# 🧹 Reactivation Sequences — Commercial Janitorial

> These are the actual messages sent to your CLIENT'S dead leads.
> Key angle: RECURRING CONTRACTS. One win = monthly revenue for years.
> Secondary angle: FM turnover — the person who rejected you may not be there anymore.

---

## SMS Sequence (4 Touches Over 7 Days)

### SMS 1 — Day 0 (The Check-In)
```
Hi {Lead First Name}, this is {Business Name}. We connected 
a while back about janitorial services for your building. 
Quick question — are you happy with your current cleaning 
company, or is that something worth revisiting?
```

### SMS 2 — Day 1 (The Pain Point)
```
Hey {Lead First Name}, just following up. A lot of facility 
managers we talk to aren't thrilled with their current 
cleaning crew but never got around to switching. If that's 
you, we'd love to give you a fresh proposal. No obligation 👍
```

### SMS 3 — Day 3 (The Easy Win)
```
Hi {Lead First Name} — we're taking on a few new buildings 
in {area} this month and offering a free walkthrough + 
custom quote. If you want to compare what you're paying 
now vs. what we'd charge, just reply YES. 🧹
```

### SMS 4 — Day 7 (The Breakup)
```
Hey {Lead First Name}, I'll leave you be! If you ever want 
to explore a new janitorial partner, just text {Business Name} 
at this number. We specialize in {commercial / office / facility} 
cleaning. Take care!
```

## Email Sequence (4 Touches Over 8 Days)

### Email 1 — Day 0
**Subject:** `Still happy with your cleaning company?`
```
Hi {Lead First Name},

This is {Agent Name} from {Business Name}. We reached out 
a while back about janitorial services for your facility 
and wanted to do a quick check-in.

A lot of our current clients told us they switched because 
their previous provider got complacent — inconsistent quality, 
missed areas, or just not responsive anymore.

If any of that sounds familiar, we'd love to put together 
a fresh proposal. No obligation — just a comparison.

Reply "interested" and I'll set it up.

Best,
{Agent Name}
{Business Name}
```

### Email 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** `How much should you be paying for janitorial?`
```
Hi {Lead First Name},

Quick thought — when's the last time you benchmarked your 
cleaning costs? A lot of facilities are overpaying simply 
because they signed their contract years ago and never 
re-evaluated.

We offer free walkthrough assessments where we'll evaluate 
your space and give you an honest quote. Even if you 
decide to stay with your current company, at least you'll 
know where you stand.

Interested? Just reply.

{Agent Name}
{Business Name}
```

### Email 3 — Day 5
**Subject:** `New buildings in {area} — your facility?`
```
Hi {Lead First Name},

We're onboarding a few new properties in your area this 
month and I wanted to reach out one more time.

If you're open to it, we can do a quick walkthrough of 
your facility and have a custom proposal to you within 
48 hours. No commitment.

Just let me know!

{Agent Name}
```

### Email 4 — Day 8
**Subject:** `Last reach-out from {Business Name} 👋`
```
Hi {Lead First Name},

This is my final follow-up. If your cleaning situation 
is sorted, that's great — I won't bother you again.

But if something changes down the road (contracts expire, 
quality dips, new building needs), keep our number handy. 
We'd love to earn your business.

Wishing you and your team the best.

{Agent Name}
{Business Name}
```

---

## Response Handling Rules

| Response | Action |
|----------|--------|
| "Yes" / "Interested" / "Send a proposal" | Forward to client's team. Include building address if available. |
| "Happy with current" | Respect it. Tag as SATISFIED. Re-contact in 6 months (contract renewal timing). |
| "Not the right person" / "I left that role" | Ask: "Who handles facilities now?" If they give a name, add as new contact. |
| "STOP" / "Unsubscribe" | Remove immediately. |
| No response after full sequence | Mark as EXHAUSTED. Re-contact in 90 days. |

---

## Timing Notes
- **Best SMS send times:** Monday-Wednesday, 8AM-10AM (FM planning time)
- **Seasonal priority:** January (new year budgets), July (mid-year contract reviews), October (year-end planning)
- **Key insight:** Target facilities with 3+ year old contracts — highest switch probability
- **FM turnover hook:** If lead data is 12+ months old, assume the FM may have changed. Adjust messaging: "We connected with your building a while back — is there a new facilities manager we should speak with?"
- **Max daily sends per client:** 50 leads/day
